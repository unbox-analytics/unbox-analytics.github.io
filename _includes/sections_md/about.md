## About

Unbox Analytics was created with a simple goal: **making data easier to understand and more useful for everyday business decisions.**

With over 15 years of experience working with data, we build practical analytics solutions using tools like Excel, SQL, Power BI, and Power Query. Our focus is on helping smaller businesses solve problems caused by inefficient reporting, disconnected spreadsheets, and unclear data processes — without expensive enterprise tools.

We believe effective analytics should be clear, practical, and genuinely useful. Whether it is cleaning data, automating reports, analyzing trends, or building dashboards, the goal is always to create solutions that save time and support better decision-making.
