<h2 style="color: #d4a574; font-family: Montserrat, sans-serif;">📊 Our story</h2>

Unbox Analytics was created with a simple goal: **making data easier to understand and more useful for everyday business decisions.**

With over 15 years of experience working with data, I build practical analytics solutions using tools like **Excel, SQL, Power BI, and Power Query**. My focus is on helping smaller businesses solve problems caused by inefficient reporting, disconnected spreadsheets, and unclear data processes — without the need for expensive enterprise tools.

I believe effective analytics should be clear, practical, and genuinely useful. Whether it's cleaning data, automating reports, analyzing trends, or building dashboards, the goal is always to create solutions that save time and support better decision-making.

This site showcases ongoing projects, learning, and practical work in data analytics and business reporting.
